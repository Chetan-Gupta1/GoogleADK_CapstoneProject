📖 User Guide & Workflow

How NovaRetail Works

Follow this 5-step process to transform raw Excel retail data into a polished, board-ready PowerPoint presentation.

1

Select or Upload Excel Dataset

Data Ingestion

The web app is ready out-of-the-box with the bundled dataset (novaretail_dataset.xlsx). To use your own data, click "📂 Change / Upload Excel ▾", choose your .xlsx file, and click "⬆️ Upload Excel".

💡 Schema requirements: Custom workbooks must contain 3 sheets (Regional_Performance, Customer_Experience, Operational_Risk) structured across a 4-region × 4-quarter grid.
2

Analyze Dataset & Compute KPIs

Automated Analytics

Click "1. Analyze Data". Python and pandas will calculate quarterly revenue totals, QoQ growth rates, mean regional Net Promoter Scores (NPS), delivery times, and identify both the top growth region and key operational risk areas.

📊 Outcome: 5 executive KPI metric tiles will populate with calculated figures and trend badges.
3

Generate AI Executive Narrative

Gemini Generation

Click "2. AI Narrative". Google Gemini evaluates the verified metrics against the PowerPoint master template to author slide-by-slide executive copy: strategic titles, summaries, key insights, and prioritized recommendations.

📄 Outcome: A 5-slide preview appears in the left workspace matching the PowerPoint deck layout.
4

Customize via AI Copilot Chat

In-Session Memory

Review the narrative in the preview cards. If you want to change the tone, emphasize specific markets (e.g., APAC expansion), or revise recommendations, use the sticky 💬 AI Copilot on the right.

Click suggestion chips or type your own instructions. Gemini updates the slide cards live and preserves your modifications across multiple conversation turns.

🔄 Session control: Click "Clear Chat" in the chat header anytime to reset the conversation history.
5

Build & Download Presentation (.pptx)

PowerPoint Automation

Once satisfied with the narrative, click "🚀 Build Presentation" from any location (top navigation bar, chat footer, or narrative banner).

An animated progress modal appears as PowerPoint slides are assembled, charts are updated, and shapes are filled with your exact customized copy.

📥 Outcome: Click "📥 Download Presentation (.pptx)" to save your executive slide deck.
